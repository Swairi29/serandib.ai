Serandib is a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system built over annual reports published by companies listed on the Colombo Stock Exchange (CSE). The corpus spans 40+ PDF filings across the banking, diversified-conglomerates, and manufacturing sectors from 2021–2024, enabling natural language queries over structured financial disclosures — questions like "What was Commercial Bank's non-performing loan ratio in 2023?" or "How did JKH's revenue compare across sectors in Q4 2024?"

The pipeline is evaluated end-to-end using RAGAS metrics — faithfulness, answer relevancy, and context precision — with results benchmarked across chunking strategies and retrieval configurations. Domain and evaluation methodology were chosen to mirror real-world financial AI use cases, particularly NL-to-structured-data retrieval in capital market environments.
